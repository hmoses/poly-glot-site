1. Google Search Console Required

1 Open Google Search Console

Go to:

https://search.google.com/search-console

Sign in with your Google account.

2 Add Property
  1. Click "Add property" (top-left dropdown)
  2. Choose "URL prefix"
  3. Enter your site URL:
https://hmoses.github.io/poly-glot-site/

Click Continue.

3 Verify Ownership

Google will offer verification methods. The easiest for GitHub Pages:

  1. Choose "HTML tag" method
  2. Google will give you a meta tag like:
    <meta name="google-site-verification" content="abc123...">
  3. Copy the entire tag
  4. Open your repo: Edit index.html on GitHub
  5. Paste it in the <head> section (right after the existing meta tags, ~line 25)
  6. Commit → wait ~1 min for GitHub Pages deploy
  7. Go back to Search Console → click "Verify"
⚠️ Do NOT delete the verification meta tag after verifying — Google re-checks periodically.
4 Submit Sitemap
  1. In Search Console, go to Sitemaps (left sidebar)
  2. Enter:
https://hmoses.github.io/poly-glot-site/sitemap.xml
  1. Click "Submit"
  2. Status should show "Success" (may take a few minutes)
5 Request Indexing
  1. In Search Console, go to URL Inspection (top search bar)
  2. Enter your homepage URL:
https://hmoses.github.io/poly-glot-site/
  1. Click "Request Indexing"
  2. Repeat for these pages:
https://hmoses.github.io/poly-glot-site/connect.html
https://hmoses.github.io/poly-glot-site/support.html

2. Bing Webmaster Tools Recommended

6 Open Bing Webmaster Tools
https://www.bing.com/webmasters

Sign in with your Microsoft account.

7 Add & Verify Site
  1. Click "Add Site"
  2. Enter:
https://hmoses.github.io/poly-glot-site/
  1. Fastest method: If you already verified Google Search Console, choose "Import from Google Search Console" — one click
  2. Alternative: Use the XML file method — download the BingSiteAuth.xml file and upload to your repo root
8 Submit Sitemap
  1. Go to Sitemaps in Bing Webmaster
  2. Submit:
https://hmoses.github.io/poly-glot-site/sitemap.xml

3. Monitor (After 1–2 Weeks) Recommended

9 Check Indexed Pages

In Google Search Console → Coverage or Pages:

  • Expect 5 pages indexed (index, connect, privacy, terms, support)
  • Check for crawl errors — fix any 404s
  • Ensure no pages are "Excluded" unexpectedly

Quick check — search Google for:

site:hmoses.github.io/poly-glot-site/
10 Review Search Performance

In Search Console → Performance:

  • Impressions — How often your site appears in search results
  • Clicks — How many people clicked through
  • CTR — Click-through rate (target: >3% for branded, >1% for generic)
  • Queries — What people searched to find you

Key queries to watch for:

  • "poly-glot ai" / "polyglot ai workspace"
  • "compare chatgpt claude gemini"
  • "ai prompt templates"
  • "multi ai app" / "compare ai answers"
