🔌 What is MCP?

MCP (Model Context Protocol) lets AI assistants like Claude and ChatGPT connect to external tools. When you connect Poly-Glot via MCP, your AI can search templates, build prompts, and compare answers across providers — all from within your chat.

❓ FAQ

Is it free?

Yes — 25 featured templates are free forever. Pro unlocks all 1,000+ templates for $9.99/month or $79.99/year (save 33%). Includes a 3-day free trial.

Do I need the iOS/macOS app?

No. MCP works independently — connect directly from Claude or ChatGPT. The app is optional for browsing templates visually and managing your subscription.

Is my data private?

Yes. Poly-Glot doesn't store your prompts or conversations. Template data flows directly between you and your AI assistant. See our Privacy Policy.

What AI providers work with Compare Mode?

Compare across any 2–7 providers: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Groq, Copilot, and Perplexity.
