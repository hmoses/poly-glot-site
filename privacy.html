Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Poly-Glot AI Workspace is developed by Harold Moses.

Information We Collect

App history and settings may be stored on your device. When you use connected AI services or the remote Poly-Glot MCP server, requests are transmitted to the selected services. The MCP backend verifies account entitlements and records operational metadata such as tool usage and errors; a signed-in account is required for trial activation and restricted Send actions. Do not submit passwords, API keys, or private receipts in support messages.

In-App Purchases

Apple processes in-app subscription payments. Poly-Glot may verify and record subscription entitlement state or Apple transaction identifiers to determine feature access; payment card details are handled by Apple, not by this website.

Third-Party Services

This website uses Google Analytics and a TikTok advertising pixel. Website visitors may have browser and interaction data processed by these services under their own policies. AI providers, optional custom-model endpoints, speech-recognition services, and audio/transcription providers may process content when you choose to send it. Their separate privacy terms apply.

Optional In-App Usage Analytics

Share six feature-use events to improve Poly-Glot. Prompts and AI responses are never sent. Change this any time.

Usage analytics in the iOS and macOS apps are disabled by default. If you turn them on in the app's About tab, Poly-Glot shares a randomly generated app-instance identifier, the app platform, the name of the AI service opened (if applicable), and six feature-use events with PostHog, a U.S.-hosted analytics processor: trial activation, first successful AI send, AI provider opened, return from an AI provider, Compare Mode enabled, and use of the daily free send. We do not include prompts, AI responses, your Apple ID, payment details, or advertising identifiers. These events are used only to understand and improve app features, not for targeted advertising or cross-company tracking.

You can turn off future analytics collection at any time in the app's About tab. The app removes its locally stored anonymous analytics identifier when you turn analytics off. Previously sent events are not deleted automatically. Contact us at the address below for privacy questions or data requests. Learn more about the processor in PostHog's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.
