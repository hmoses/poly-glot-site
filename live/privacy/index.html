Poly-Glot AI Live Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026 · Applies to the separate Poly-Glot AI Live app for iOS, iPadOS and macOS.

Poly-Glot AI Live is designed to translate user-selected speech or typed text without a Poly-Glot cloud account.

Speech, microphone, and translation

When you start listening, or enable optional hands-free mode, the app requests microphone and speech-recognition access to transcribe speech. Microphone use depends on your controls, permissions, foreground state, device capabilities and audio route. Speech recognition may use Apple's speech services or on-device processing, depending on available system features. Apple's translation framework may require downloadable language resources.

What stays on your device

The app does not save the captured audio as an audio recording. Conversation text is kept in memory for the current session, and the app uses local device preferences for language choices and listening settings. Poly-Glot AI Live does not operate a developer-managed transcript account or upload conversation transcripts to a Poly-Glot server in this release.

Exporting information

If you choose to copy or share a transcript, you control the destination. Shared content is then subject to the privacy practices of the service or person you select. Check transcripts before sharing sensitive information.

Analytics, advertising, and billing

This app version does not include developer-run behavioral analytics, advertising SDKs, or subscription billing. Apple may process information to operate Apple platform services and may provide aggregated App Store diagnostics subject to Apple's privacy terms.

Your choices

You can decline or revoke microphone and speech-recognition permissions using Apple Settings or System Settings, turn off hands-free listening, and clear the current conversation from within the app. You can stop using the app and remove it to delete locally stored preferences. Copies you previously exported must be managed at their destinations.

Contact

For product questions or privacy inquiries, use the Poly-Glot AI Live support page and specify this app.

Important distinction

This policy applies to Poly-Glot AI Live only, not to Poly-Glot AI Workspace or any other app.
