Poly-Glot AI Live Support

Information for the iPhone, iPad and macOS versions of Poly-Glot AI Live.

How do I translate a conversation?

Choose Your language and Other language, then open More options to type or use the manual microphone controls. The app checks whether your device supports the selected translation pair. If a pair is unsupported, try another supported pair or download language resources when prompted.

How do AirPods and automatic listening work?

Connect compatible headphones, allow microphone and speech-recognition permissions, choose the language you expect to hear, then enable automatic listening. It runs only when the device, language pair, permissions, and audio route support the feature. It does not automatically identify any possible spoken language.

How do I show subtitles or export a conversation?

Enable Show live subtitles to display available translated speech. The Conversation transcript shows captured turns; when transcript content exists, you can copy or share it using the operating system's share controls.

What languages are supported?

The interface offers 38 display languages. Speech recognition and translation are separate system capabilities, and their available languages and pairs depend on the Apple device and OS.

How do I reset microphone access?

Open your Apple device's Settings or System Settings, find Privacy & Security, and review Microphone and Speech Recognition permissions for Poly-Glot AI Live.

What if something is inaccurate?

AI-assisted speech recognition and translation can make mistakes. Verify important conversations. Meeting action or decision suggestions are heuristic highlights, not verified meeting minutes.

Need more help?

Visit the Poly-Glot support page for contact options, and specify Poly-Glot AI Live and your iOS/macOS version.
