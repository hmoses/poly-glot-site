# Poly-Glot Privacy, Free Trial and Pricing

Direct answers about Poly-Glot AI Workspace privacy, third-party providers, free limits and subscriptions.

## Does Poly-Glot store my conversations?

App history and settings can remain on-device. Submitted prompts go to the selected AI provider, while remote MCP requests pass through the Poly-Glot server. The MCP service verifies entitlements and records operational usage metadata; processing providers have their own privacy policies.

## How does Photo to AI handle my photos?

On iPhone and iPad, Photo to AI uses Optical Character Recognition (OCR) to turn writing from a photo into editable text on the device. Users can take or import a photo and add optional text instructions. Poly-Glot does not upload those photos to its own server. A photo leaves the device only when the user explicitly chooses a destination from Apple's Share Sheet or manually attaches it inside a third-party AI app. Recipients may process or retain photos and instructions under their own policies. All eight Poly-Glot providers support text workflows; photo and text attachments are not guaranteed across all eight apps or their selected models. If a provider does not appear in the Share Sheet, attach the image manually within that provider if its interface allows it. Photo to AI is included during the existing three-day trial and requires Pro afterward.

## How does iPhone/iPad Photo to AI handle photos and privacy?

OCR (optical character recognition) extracts editable text from a photo on-device. On iPhone and iPad, you can take or choose an image, add optional text instructions, and open the system Share Sheet to share with a compatible recipient app. **Poly-Glot does not automatically upload photos to its own servers.** When you choose an app, the image and text may be processed or stored by that app under its privacy policy. Photo-plus-text intake is not guaranteed for all eight AI services. The eight AI providers remain available for text prompts.

Photo to AI is included in the full-access 3-day trial and requires Pro once that trial expires; an app-managed trial does not automatically create a paid subscription.

## Does optional analytics collect prompts or AI responses?

No. With consent in iOS or macOS Settings, optional PostHog telemetry sends limited usage events but not prompt text or AI responses. Users can disable analytics in Settings. MCP operational metadata logging and website analytics/advertising pixels are separate. See the [Privacy Policy](https://hmoses.github.io/poly-glot-site/privacy.html).

## What happens to voice input?

Voice input can use on-device recognition where supported, with a server-based fallback depending on the device and settings. The MCP `transcribe_audio` tool sends supplied audio to its configured transcription provider and requires active trial or Pro access.

## How long is the full-access trial?

The app-managed 3-day full-access trial begins with the first eligible Send for a verified account. It does not automatically convert into a paid Apple subscription. Starting the trial and restoring a paid subscription require appropriate account verification.

## What happens when the trial ends?

After trial expiry, 25 free templates remain available, with one single-AI Send shared across free workflows every rolling 24 hours. Pro templates, Compare Mode, BYOM execution and MCP language-processing tools require an active trial or Pro; the trial itself never starts a paid subscription.
