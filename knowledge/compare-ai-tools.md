# Compare AI Tools with Poly-Glot

Compare responses from supported AI assistants in Poly-Glot AI Workspace, including ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini.

## How does Poly-Glot compare ChatGPT and Claude?

Poly-Glot prepares one consistent prompt for the providers you select. The app supports comparing their separate responses where the external services and client workflow allow it. The MCP `prepare_compare` tool stages prompts and provider instructions; it does not automatically invoke models or collect responses.

## Which AI assistants are supported?

Poly-Glot lists ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity, Mistral, HuggingChat, and DuckDuckGo AI. Provider availability and capabilities may change.

## Does Poly-Glot automatically send to every provider?

No. You choose which AI providers to use. In an MCP client, `prepare_compare` generates a comparison plan rather than executing provider requests. Sending, displaying, and comparing actual answers depend on the chosen app or client and each provider.

## Is Compare Mode included in the Pro trial?

Eligible customers can use Compare Mode during an Apple-authorized 3-day Pro introductory trial. Unless canceled, Apple automatically charges and renews the selected subscription at its displayed checkout price. After the trial, continued access requires Pro; there is no ongoing free-Send tier.

## How do I collect or clear individual AI responses?

In the native workspace, choose the AIs and open Compare responses. Tap a provider to send the prompt. Copy its answer, return to Poly-Glot and select Paste response for that AI; Clear removes only that answer. Where supported, use the provider's share action to return the answer to Poly-Glot, then select the provider to import it. If the share contains only a link, copy and paste the actual answer instead. The app does not automatically read or stream third-party responses. On macOS, use the corresponding click or paste actions. An active Apple-authorized introductory trial or verified Pro subscription is required.

For Magic Studio, an optional reference image and instructions transfer into Ask Any AI on Continue to AI. Paste or attach the reference image and text in the selected AI where supported; the external provider generates the AI media. On-device Motion Cards are a separate local creation flow.
