# Compare AI Tools with Poly-Glot

Compare responses from supported AI assistants in Poly-Glot AI Workspace, including ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini.

## How does Poly-Glot compare ChatGPT and Claude?

Poly-Glot lets you prepare the same prompt for selected AI assistants and compare their responses. It does not merge providers into one model or guarantee identical response behavior.

## Which AI assistants are supported?

Poly-Glot lists ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity, Copilot, Mistral, HuggingChat, and DuckDuckGo AI. Provider availability and capabilities may change.

## Does Poly-Glot automatically send to every provider?

No. You choose the providers and follow the available sending workflow. The external AI services handle their own responses.

## Can I compare AI answers for free?

Compare Mode is available during the full-access trial and requires Pro afterward. Free access after trial is limited; consult the current product or App Store for entitlements.
