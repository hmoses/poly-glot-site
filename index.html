❓ FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know about Poly-Glot AI Workspace.

What is Poly-Glot AI Workspace?+

Poly-Glot AI Workspace is a native app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac that lets you ask any AI — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity, Copilot, Mistral, and more — all from one place. Type or speak in any of 38 languages, use 1,000+ ready-to-use templates, and compare answers from multiple AIs side by side. No API keys needed — everything runs on your device.

When will Poly-Glot AI Workspace be available?+

Poly-Glot AI Workspace is currently in review with Apple and will be available on the App Store for iPhone, iPad, and Mac very soon. Check back here for updates!

Is my data private?+

Yes. Poly-Glot AI Workspace runs 100% on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, prompts, or usage information. No analytics, no tracking, no servers. Your prompts never leave your device.

What is Compare Mode?+

Compare Mode lets you send the same prompt to multiple AI models at once — like ChatGPT and Claude — and see both responses side by side. Pick the best answer instead of guessing which AI to use. Compare Mode is free for all users.

Which AI models does it support?+

Poly-Glot AI Workspace works with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity, Copilot, Mistral, Groq, and more. Your prompt is automatically copied to your clipboard and the AI opens instantly — some even auto-fill your prompt.

How does the free trial work?+

New users get a 3-day free trial with access to 25 featured templates. After 3 days, upgrade to Pro ($9.99/mo or $79.99/yr) to unlock all 1,000+ templates, custom template building, and all future content.

How many input methods are supported?+

Five: Type your prompt from scratch, paste existing text, import documents or files, scan printed or handwritten text with your camera, or dictate using on-device speech recognition in 38 languages.

Can I cancel my subscription?+

Yes. All subscriptions are managed through Apple and can be cancelled anytime from your Apple ID settings. No questions asked, no hoops to jump through.
