❓ FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about Poly-Glot AI Workspace.

What is Poly-Glot AI Workspace?+

Poly-Glot AI Workspace is a native app for Mac, iPhone & iPad that gives you one workspace for 9 supported AI services: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity, Copilot, Mistral, HuggingChat, and DuckDuckGo AI. The UI is localized in 38 languages, includes 1,000+ prompt templates, supports typing, pasting, importing, scanning, and dictation, and lets you choose an output language. Compare Mode helps you send the same prompt to multiple supported AI services so you can compare the results. No API keys are required for the native app workflow.

Where is Poly-Glot AI Workspace available?+

Available now on the App Store for Mac, iPhone, and iPad. You can also connect via MCP — see the Connect section.

Is my data private?+

Poly-Glot is designed to minimize data collection and keeps app history and settings on your device. When you choose an AI service and send a prompt, that prompt is sent to the selected third-party AI provider so it can generate a response. Poly-Glot does not claim that prompts remain exclusively on-device once you send them to an external AI service.

What is Compare Mode?+

Compare Mode lets you reuse the same prompt across multiple supported AI services so you can review different responses and choose the one you prefer. In the current native app workflow, each selected AI opens separately rather than all providers rendering inside one combined response pane. Compare Mode is available during the free trial and with Pro.

Which AI services does it support?+

Poly-Glot supports 9 AI services: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity, Copilot, Mistral, HuggingChat, and DuckDuckGo AI.

How does the free trial work?+

Poly-Glot includes a 3-day free trial with Pro features unlocked. After the trial, free access is limited and Pro removes those limits. Current subscription pricing and entitlement details are shown in the App Store and in the app at purchase time.

How many input methods are supported?+

Five: type, paste, import, scan, and dictate. The entire UI is fully localized in 38 languages.

Can I cancel my subscription?+

Yes. Subscriptions are managed through Apple and can be canceled from your Apple subscription settings. Your access remains subject to Apple's subscription terms and the current billing period.

Why use 1 AI when you can compare 9?+

Poly-Glot gives you one workspace for 9 supported AI services, with Compare Mode to reuse the same prompt across providers and review multiple answers. Poly-Glot does not include or replace any separate paid subscription, account, or usage limits required by a third-party AI provider.

What is MCP and how does it work with Poly-Glot?+

MCP (Model Context Protocol) lets compatible AI assistants connect to external tools. Poly-Glot's MCP server can expose capabilities such as searching templates, retrieving template details, building prompts, preparing comparisons, checking language options, and opening the workspace from supported MCP clients.
