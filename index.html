❓ FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know about Poly-Glot.

What is Poly-Glot AI Workspace? +

Poly-Glot AI Workspace is a native iOS and macOS app that lets you ask any AI — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, and more — from one place. Type or speak in any of 38 languages, use 1,000+ ready-to-use templates, compare answers from multiple AIs side by side, and pick the best one. No API keys needed — everything runs on your device.

Is my data private? +

Yes. Poly-Glot runs 100% on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, prompts, or usage information. No analytics, no tracking, no servers.

How does the free trial work? +

New users get a 3-day free trial that includes access to 25 featured templates. The trial starts when you first use a template. After 3 days, you can upgrade to Pro to unlock all 1,000+ templates. The trial is tied to your Apple ID.

Does it work with ChatGPT, Claude, and other AI models? +

Yes. Poly-Glot works with every major AI. Send your prompt directly to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity, Copilot, Mistral, and more. Use Compare Mode to ask multiple AIs at once and see which gives the best answer.

How many input methods are supported? +

Five: Type your prompt from scratch, paste existing text, import documents or files, scan printed or handwritten text with your camera, or dictate using on-device speech recognition. All methods produce structured, copy-ready prompts.

Can I cancel my subscription? +

Yes. All subscriptions are managed through Apple and can be cancelled anytime from your Apple ID settings. No questions asked.
