❓ FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know about Poly-Glot AI Workspace.

What is Poly-Glot AI Workspace?+

Poly-Glot AI Workspace is a native app for Mac, iPhone & iPad that lets you ask any AI — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity, Copilot, Mistral, and more — all from one place. The entire UI is fully localized in 38 languages. Type or speak naturally, use 1,000+ templates, and toggle 🔀 Compare Mode to send to multiple AIs and pick the best answer. Choose your output language — every response comes back in your selected language from the first word. No API keys needed — everything runs on your device.

Where is Poly-Glot AI Workspace available?+

Available now on the App Store for Mac, iPhone, and iPad. You can also connect via MCP — see the Connect section.

Is my data private?+

Yes. 100% on your device. No data collection, no tracking, no servers. Your prompts never leave your device.

What is Compare Mode?+

Compare Mode lets you send the same prompt to multiple AI models at once and see both responses side by side. Pick the best answer. Included in the free trial and Pro.

Which AI models does it support?+

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity, Copilot, Mistral, HuggingChat, DuckDuckGo AI.

How does the free trial work?+

3-day trial with everything unlocked. After that, 1 free send/day. Upgrade to Pro ($9.99/mo or $79.99/yr) for unlimited.

How many input methods are supported?+

Five: type, paste, import, scan, and dictate. The entire UI is fully localized in 38 languages.

Can I cancel my subscription?+

Yes. Cancel anytime from Apple ID settings. No questions asked.

Why use 1 AI when you can compare 9?+

Most AI subscriptions cost $20/mo for one service. Poly-Glot gives you 9 AIs with Compare Mode from $9.99/mo. Fully localized in 38 languages.

What is MCP and how does it work with Poly-Glot?+

MCP (Model Context Protocol) lets AI assistants connect to external tools. Connect Poly-Glot via MCP to search templates and build prompts from within Claude or ChatGPT.
