Ask Any AI

Ask Any AI lets you type or speak a question in any of 38 supported languages and send it to one or more AI apps. Poly-Glot does not process your prompts — it routes them directly to the AI app you select.

Two language settings:

  • App Language — Changes the entire Poly-Glot interface (menus, buttons, template names) to your preferred language.
  • AI Output Language — Tells the AI which language to respond in. You can write your prompt in any language and have the AI respond in a different one.

Ask Any AI is free during the 3-day trial. After the trial, it requires a Pro subscription.

Compare Mode

Compare Mode lets you send the same prompt to multiple AI apps simultaneously. Each AI opens separately so you can compare responses side by side. Available with both Ask Any AI and templates.
