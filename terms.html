What Poly-Glot Does

Poly-Glot AI Workspace is a prompt-building and AI-routing app for iOS and macOS. You type or speak a question — or choose from 1,000+ expert prompt templates — then send it to any of 9 AI apps: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Grok, Microsoft Copilot, Mistral, DuckDuckGo, or HuggingChat. Poly-Glot does not process your prompts — it copies them to your clipboard and opens the AI you select.

How Sending to AI Works

When you tap Send and pick an AI:

  • ChatGPT, Perplexity, Grok — Your prompt is copied to your clipboard and the AI opens with your prompt already filled in. No paste needed.
  • Claude, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, Mistral, DuckDuckGo, HuggingChat — Your prompt is copied to your clipboard and the AI opens. Paste your prompt to send it.

Two Language Settings

  • App Language — Changes the entire Poly-Glot interface (menus, buttons, template names) to your preferred language. 37 languages supported.
  • AI Output Language — Tells the AI which language to respond in. You can write your prompt in any language and have the AI respond in a different one.

AI Routing & Privacy

Poly-Glot routes your prompts to third-party AI services via their free web tiers. We do not store, transmit, or process your prompts on any server. Everything stays on your device. Each AI app has its own terms and privacy policy.
